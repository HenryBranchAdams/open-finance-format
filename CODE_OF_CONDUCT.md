# Code of conduct

OFF is a technical commons. Participate with care for the people doing the work
and for the downstream users who may rely on precise protocol language.

Expected conduct:

- discuss evidence, behavior, and tradeoffs rather than attacking people;
- make room for newcomers and explain project-specific context;
- disclose conflicts of interest and distinguish fact from inference;
- respect privacy, attribution, licenses, and responsible security disclosure;
- accept correction and de-escalate when a discussion stops being productive;
  and
- do not harass, threaten, discriminate, impersonate, or publish another
  person's private information.

The maintainer may edit, hide, or remove contributions; pause a discussion; or
temporarily or permanently restrict participation when needed to protect the
project or its participants. Report conduct concerns through a private channel
described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) when one is available. If only a public
route exists, request private contact without naming people or exposing details.

Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately, and
shared only as necessary. The current founder-maintainer is the final appeal
point; OFF does not yet have an independent conduct committee.
