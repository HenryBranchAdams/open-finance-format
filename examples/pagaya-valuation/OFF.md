# Pagaya Technologies valuation — Q2 2026 risk-retention FCFE refresh

This OFF package values Pagaya Technologies Ltd. (`PGY`) as of 2026-07-30 after the company filed its Q2 2026 earnings release and shareholder letter. The formula-driven six-year equity cash-flow model starts with Network Volume, converts volume to fees less production costs (`FRLPC`), normalizes owner earnings, and deducts the equity capital required to support incremental risk-retention assets.

## What changed from the pre-earnings model

- 2026 Network Volume now uses management's revised $12.50–$13.25 billion guidance range, with the base at the $12.875 billion midpoint.
- 2026 normalized net income is calibrated to management's $155–$180 million GAAP net-income guidance range.
- Base FRLPC falls to 4.3% from the prior 4.65% assumption, reflecting the 4.2% Q2 result.
- Valuation diluted shares rise to 97.25 million from 86.0 million, using Q2 weighted-average diluted shares.
- Base required equity for incremental volume falls modestly to 1.25% from 1.5%. Sequential investments increased $98.8 million while secured borrowing increased $96.7 million, but the retained-capital assumption remains analyst judgment.

## What proves or breaks the thesis

Proof would be Network Volume tracking within the raised range, FRLPC stabilizing in the low-to-mid-4% range, and recurring GAAP profitability converting to cash without a disproportionate increase in net retained-credit capital. Q2 record ABS funding of $3.7 billion and near-flat sequential net risk assets support that case.

The thesis breaks if funding costs push FRLPC below 4%, credit performance requires materially more equity support than modeled, or volume growth fails to produce normalized owner-earnings leverage.

## Method and evidence boundary

For each scenario, Network Volume multiplied by FRLPC percentage produces FRLPC. Normalized net income is a scenario percentage of FRLPC. D&A and capex are normalized to 5% and 4% of FRLPC, respectively. FCFE equals normalized net income plus D&A less capex and required equity capital, with required equity capital modeled as a percentage of incremental Network Volume.

The model uses scenario-specific costs of equity and perpetual growth: 16.0% / 2.5% downside, 14.0% / 3.0% base, and 12.5% / 3.5% upside. The resulting equity value is divided by 97.25 million valuation diluted shares. Forward P/E and a fee-platform SOTP are corroborative screens only.
