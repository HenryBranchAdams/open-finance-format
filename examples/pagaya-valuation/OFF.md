# Pagaya Technologies valuation — risk-retention FCFE

This OFF package values Pagaya Technologies Ltd. (`PGY`) as of 2026-07-23 with a formula-driven six-year equity cash-flow model. The engine starts with Network Volume, converts volume to fees less production costs (`FRLPC`), normalizes owner earnings, and deducts the equity capital required to support incremental risk-retention assets.

This reverse-DCF result is a model-derived inference under the named operating and terminal assumptions; it is not quoted consensus or a unique statement of market belief. The near-term debate is therefore less about headline volume growth and more about whether FRLPC remains near the mid-4% range while incremental risk-retention equity stays contained.

## What proves or breaks the thesis

Proof would be 2026 Network Volume tracking toward the upper half of management's $11.45–$13.0 billion range, FRLPC holding near 4.6%, and recurring GAAP profitability converting to cash without a disproportionate increase in retained-credit capital. The $750 million auto ABS transaction announced on 2026-07-16 supports the funding-execution case, but the next proof point is Q2 2026 results on 2026-07-30.

The thesis breaks if funding costs compress FRLPC, credit performance requires materially more equity support than modeled, or volume growth fails to produce normalized owner-earnings leverage.

## Method

For each scenario, Network Volume multiplied by FRLPC percentage produces FRLPC. Normalized net income is a scenario percentage of FRLPC. D&A and capex are normalized to 5% and 4% of FRLPC, respectively. FCFE equals normalized net income plus D&A less capex and required equity capital, with required equity capital modeled as a percentage of incremental Network Volume.

The model uses scenario-specific costs of equity and perpetual growth: 16.0% / 2.5% downside, 14.0% / 3.0% base, and 12.5% / 3.5% upside. The resulting equity value is divided by 86.0 million valuation diluted shares. Forward P/E and a fee-platform SOTP are corroborative screens only.
