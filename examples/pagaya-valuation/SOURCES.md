## Earnings quality, funding, and credit

Adjusted net income exceeded GAAP attributable income by $55.719 in Q2. The largest exclusions were $37.972 of investment impairment, $8.569 of share-based compensation, $5.581 of capitalized-software and other asset write-offs, and $3.383 of non-recurring expenses. These items make adjusted EPS a less conservative anchor than GAAP guidance for this model.

Pagaya reported record Q2 ABS funding of $3.7 billion across six transactions. Sequentially, investments in loans and securities rose $98.751 while secured borrowing rose $96.720, leaving net risk assets nearly flat. That supports a modest reduction in the base incremental-equity assumption, but it does not prove the assumption because portfolio mix, back-book distributions, resecuritizations, and other financing flows also moved.

Management said Q2 Network Volume grew 33% year over year, led by Auto, and that credit remained within expectations. FRLPC declined 61 basis points to 4.2% because newer products and partners begin at lower margins and elevated benchmark rates compressed funding economics.
