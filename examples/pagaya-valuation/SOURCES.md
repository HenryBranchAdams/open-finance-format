## Forecast assumption boundary

The base 2026 Network Volume assumption is the midpoint of management's guidance. Later Network Volume, FRLPC, normalized net income as a percentage of FRLPC, cost of equity, terminal growth, and required risk-retention equity are analyst judgment—not company guidance or consensus.

All cases assume D&A equal to 5% of FRLPC and capex equal to 4% of FRLPC. Required equity capital on incremental Network Volume is 2.0% in the downside case, 1.5% in the base case, and 1.25% in the upside case.

The reverse DCF holds the base operating path and 3.0% terminal growth constant while varying cost of equity. It indicates that the frozen market price is consistent with approximately a 17.6% cost of equity. This is a model-derived inference under the stated assumptions, not quoted consensus or a unique market belief.
