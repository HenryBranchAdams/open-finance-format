# ServiceNow valuation — owner-FCFF DCF

This OFF package values ServiceNow, Inc. (`NOW`) as of 2026-07-23 using a formula-driven six-year DCF. The engine uses reported results, management's current operating evidence, and explicit analyst assumptions. It does not use analyst consensus estimates.

The stock screens modestly undervalued in the base case, but the asymmetry is not yet compelling: downside is roughly twice the base-case upside, and 79% of base enterprise value comes from the terminal value. The decision posture is therefore **watchlist / starter only, with proof required**.

The market price implies approximately 14.5% constant annual revenue growth through 2031 under the base margin and reinvestment path. The base case underwrites 17.6%. The debate is therefore not whether ServiceNow can grow in 2026—the company has already guided the year—but how gradually growth fades while stock-based compensation falls and owner economics improve.

## What proves or breaks the thesis

Proof would be current remaining performance obligation growth holding near 20%, continued progress toward management's $30 billion-plus 2030 subscription revenue target, and stock-based compensation falling below 10% of revenue by 2029 while owner-EBIT margin expands.

The thesis breaks if revenue growth falls below the market-implied path before margin expansion, deferred-revenue funding normalizes materially, or AI monetization fails to offset seat and pricing pressure.

## Method

The model starts with FY2025 reported revenue and converts non-GAAP operating income into owner EBIT by subtracting stock-based compensation. Forecast EBIT is taxed at a 21% cash tax rate, then adjusted for D&A, capital expenditures, and the change in operating net working capital to derive unlevered owner free cash flow.

All three operating cases use an 8.63% formula-driven WACC and 3.0% perpetual growth rate. A separate WACC / terminal-growth table shows valuation sensitivity. Net debt is $810 million and diluted shares are 1,034 million.

## Evidence and limits

The package is screen-grade. Only the latest full historical year is populated, and aggregate operating net working capital is used because a complete receivables / payables / deferred-revenue component history was not assembled. The frozen market price is a short-lived snapshot. OFF conformance proves package structure and declared lineage, not the correctness of the assumptions or cross-engine spreadsheet equivalence.
