## Evidence limitations

- No licensed consensus feed was available or used.
- Peer financials and market values were not independently normalized.
- Q2 2026 had not been reported at the valuation date.
- The March 2026 corporate-cash balance was adjusted only for the known June
  dividend; intervening Q2 cash generation and capital returns were unavailable.
- Historical series are reconstructed from company releases and the 2025 20-F;
  where a line was not necessary for FCFF or not comparably disclosed, the
  workbook leaves it blank rather than estimating a reported fact.
