## Evidence limitations

- No licensed consensus, peer-multiple, precedent-transaction, beta or
  enterprise-value dataset was available. EV/revenue and EV/normalized-EBITDA
  outputs are explicitly selected analyst stresses, not normalized market
  medians.
- The price snapshot is secondary and the GRSD ticker began trading only on 23
  July 2026; the reference must be refreshed before decision use.
- The exit-multiple DCF uses analyst-selected terminal multiples. The APV uses
  an illustrative debt-amortization and tax-shield schedule because no complete
  contractual amortization / refinancing schedule was available.
- A SOTP is not presented: Grandstand reports one segment and the evidence set
  does not provide separable business-line margins, reinvestment, corporate-cost
  allocations or normalized unit-specific valuation benchmarks.
- Q2 2026 had not been reported. The March cash / debt bridge does not fully
  capture later deferred-consideration settlements, restructure cash costs or
  second-quarter cash generation.
- The model uses management guidance as a starting point and does not assume the
  full benefit of the stated restructure without evidence of realization.
