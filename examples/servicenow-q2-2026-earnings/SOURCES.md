## Assumption boundary

Scenario-specific core growth, FX contribution, delayed-deal recovery, professional-services revenue, cRPO growth, operating margin, operating-to-net-income conversion, and the scenario interpretation are analyst judgment. Reported cRPO is separately judged—not a bookings, renewal, cohort, or contract-duration model—and is compared with both reported and constant-currency company guides. The workbook makes each editable or visibly sourced, records the calculation path, and centralizes comparison-only consensus inputs. The model does not claim access to company-internal bookings, analyst channel checks, or non-public information.
