Its positive EPS wedge is primarily a 27.5% non-GAAP operating-margin estimate, 100 basis points above company guidance; the assumed operating-income conversion also contributes. The workbook's first visible `Cover` sheet is the concise visual summary. It also contains editable scenario assumptions, formula-driven forecasts, a comparison-only expectation bar, model checks, a native scenario chart, and a source ledger.

## Method

The subscription forecast starts with reported Q2 2025 subscription revenue and applies scenario-specific growth built from:

1. a core constant-currency growth signal anchored to Q1 2026 subscription and cRPO growth;
2. ServiceNow's disclosed approximate 125-basis-point Q2 contribution from Armis;
3. an explicit FX overlay; and
4. a bull-only partial recovery of delayed Middle East closings.

Professional-services revenue is forecast separately. Reported cRPO is an explicitly independent scenario judgment, reconciled to prior-year cRPO and shown against both company guidance bases; it is not a bookings, contract-duration, cohort, or renewal model. Non-GAAP operating income applies a scenario margin to total revenue. Non-GAAP net income then uses a visible operating-income conversion ratio anchored to Q2 2025 and Q1 2026, and diluted EPS divides by post-split diluted shares. The workbook shows EPS at the company margin guide and at the Q1 conversion rate so the margin and conversion wedges remain visible.

Consensus estimates do not feed any forecast formula and have no lineage edge to a modeled output. They are shown only as an external comparison bar.

## Decision posture and limits

This is a moderate-confidence operating-results forecast and a **wait-for-proof** setup, not a trade instruction. It contains no event-isolating options data, positioning data, share-price reaction model, detailed contract-cohort schedule, or full below-the-line tax model. Armis integration, FX, delayed-deal timing, and Armis termination-for-convenience contract terms that limit related cRPO recognition create wider-than-normal attribution risk. The decision matrix distinguishes a quality confirmation from a margin-led but demand-in-line result.

OFF validation establishes structural conformance, resource integrity, freshness propagation, and author-declared lineage. It does not prove the forecast is correct or that spreadsheet formulas will execute identically in every engine.
